About Rudra

Rudra Gosalia is an emerging filmmaker and student who has been involved in various film and media projects in Australia. A proud graduate of the Bachelor of Film and Television program at Bond University, Rudra is part of a new wave of talent driving the flourishing local film industry.

Rudra brings a multifaceted skill set to set, with experience ranging from writing and directing his own short films like Casey's Guide to the Apocalypse, to key grip and G&E roles on other productions such as Us As I Remember and Mason Watts' Growing Pains music video. His work aims to create deeply human, authentic, and visually engaging stories.
