## 6. Bio (~105 words + button)
**Dr Anubhav Saxena** is a Sydney general practitioner and the Director of Bay
Health Clinic in Double Bay. He completed his MBBS at the University of
Sydney, alongside a Bachelor of Science (Advanced) and a Master of Philosophy
in stroke research, and holds a Diploma in Child Health and Fellowship of the
RACGP. An Honorary Associate Lecturer at Macquarie University, he has
practised in communities across Sydney — Seven Hills, Double Bay, Hoxton Park
and Hornsby — and holds special interests in skin cancer medicine,
integrative medicine and ADHD care. Away from the clinic, he donates his time
to supporting the helpless Parramatta Eels. **For an extended bio, click
below.**
